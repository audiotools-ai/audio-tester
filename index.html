📋 How to Test Your Audio & Earphones

Using our free online tool is easy. Follow these simple steps to verify your hardware:

  • Left/Right Channel Test: Click the Left or Right buttons to check if your audio channels are correctly balanced. If you click "Left" but hear sound on the right, your earphones are worn backward!
  • Stereo Balance Test: The center "Both" button plays an equal frequency audio signal to both channels to help identify if one earbud is quieter than the other.
  • Microphone Quality Check: Click the voice test button and grant microphone permissions. Speak clearly for 3 seconds. The tool automatically plays back your HD audio recording so you can listen for background static or clipping.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is my left earphone quieter than the right?

This common issue is often caused by dirt accumulation inside the earbud grid, internal wire fraying from bending, or balance settings on your phone or laptop operating system being off-center.

Is my microphone working?

Our voice recorder tests your hardware locally. If you can hear your voice clearly during the 3-second loop playback but your friends can't hear you in apps like Discord, Zoom, or TeamSpeak, check your in-app input device configurations.

Does this tool store my voice recordings?

No. Privacy is fully respected. Your microphone test is processed entirely inside your local web browser using temporary memory chunks. No audio files or data are ever uploaded to an external server.
