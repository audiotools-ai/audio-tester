❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

If your friends can't hear you on Discord but your microphone is physically plugged in, try these rapid steps:

  • Select Your Mic Manually: Open Discord, go to User Settings ➔ Voice & Video. Switch your Input Device from "Default" to the exact name of your microphone.
  • Reset Voice Settings: Scroll to the absolute bottom of the Voice & Video page and click the red **Reset Voice Settings** button.
  • Test Your Hardware: Use our free microphone tester above to record a quick 3-second clip. If it plays back clearly here, the bug is strictly inside Discord.

An uneven volume drop between your left and right channels is usually caused by two common issues:

  • Dirty Mesh Grid sound barrier: Earwax and dust block speaker grids over time. Take a clean, dry toothbrush and gently brush the mesh downward to flick debris out.
  • Shifted Audio Balance Slider: Go to your phone or computer sound settings, search for **"Audio Balance,"** and make sure the channel slider sits precisely at 50/50 in the center.
  • Test Your Stereo Balance: Click the **Left** and **Right** isolated channel test buttons above to see if it is a permanent hardware failure.
